# M3: Contextual proof lifting

Running milestone note (same convention as the retired `m2.md`): status is
updated as work proceeds; delete once absorbed into the next milestone.

## Goal

Formalize the contextual calculus `Γ | Δ ⊢ φ` from `contextual_hol.md` and
prove that its proofs lift to Core:

```text
Proves env Γ Δ φ   →   CoreThm (closure of Γ | Δ ⊢ φ)
```

Exit condition: the lifting induction closes without discovering an infinite
family.  Any stuck case becomes a named finite basis addition and feeds back
into M1/M2 (`beta_basis.cor` + `BasisMap.lean`).

## Design decisions (fixed in M3.1)

### Interpretation target: single closure, not the nested tower

The Core reading of `Γ | Δ ⊢ φ` for the lifting is the SINGLE-closure form

```text
Vy C[Γ] (chain Δ [[φ]]Γ)        (one Forall over the whole context object)
```

not the nested `closeContext` tower.  Reasoning under one abstract context
type `Y` keeps every lifting step Y-generic (`MPy`, `Forall_mono` at
`X := Y`, ...) — no per-depth axiom families.  Export to the repo-canonical
nested form (`Forall X Final (...)` etc.) is by induction on Γ from two new
Y-generic bridge facts (see "expected basis additions").

### Assumption chain: head-newest

`chain (ψ :: Δ) φ = chain Δ (ψ → φ)` — the head of Δ is the innermost
implication.  Consequence: `impIntro` (discharging the newest assumption) is
a definitional no-op at the closed level.  Note this diverges from
`Core.lean`'s `assumptionsToBody` (head-outermost); reconcile when M3.3
defines the closure function.

### Propositional layer: Hilbert-style

`hyp` + `mp` + axiom schemas (K, S, Łukasiewicz contraposition for classical
negation, ∧/∨/↔ axioms).  Each schema lifts to ONE Y-generic ∀-lifted fibre
tautology; `mp` lifts through the assumption chain by iterating Y-generic
K/S facts.  The deduction theorem is not needed (impIntro is primitive and
free).  ND-style rules are the job of the HOL→contextual elaboration
(adequacy theorem 2), not of this calculus.

### Quantifier layer: adjunction moves + generators

- `allIntro` / `exElim`: the context moves, with freshness as a structural
  name condition (`occursFree x ψ = false` for ψ ∈ Δ, resp. also for the
  ∃-elim conclusion).
- `allCounit`: over a context with head x, the shadow-weakened `∀x.φ`
  implies φ.  Its β-content is exactly `translate_substFormula` at
  `t := Term.var x` (hence its `avoids x φ` condition).
- `ctxWeaken` / `ctxSubst`: the two structurality GENERATORS (reindex along
  `snd`, resp. along `⟨t, id⟩`).  General context maps σ stay admissible by
  composition; they are not primitive.
- `allElim` is DERIVED: ctxWeaken → allCounit+mp → ctxSubst.  Checked in
  Lean (`Proves.allElim`), exercised by a corecheck-style example
  (`⊢ (∀u. R(u,c)) → R(c,c)`).
- `exIntro` is PRIMITIVE with `substFormula` in the premise.  Named-syntax
  asymmetry: the dual unit route would substitute through `φ → ∃x.φ`, where
  x occurs both free and bound, which the `avoids` discipline of
  `substFormula` forbids.  Not a β-basis gap — a syntax-representation fact.

### ctxSubst substitutes only the conclusion

Assumptions must satisfy `occursFree x ψ = false` and pass through
UNCHANGED.  Rationale: the standard use (eliminating `∀x.φ` sitting in Δ)
has Δ containing a binder for x, so a pointwise `substFormula` on Δ (with
its `avoids x ψ` requirement) is unsatisfiable.  Core-side obligation for
the assumptions is the weakening collapse `snd ∘ ⟨t, id⟩ = id`, not
substitution.  The general "substitute Δ too" rule is admissible via
impIntro → ctxSubst → mp/hyp.

### Well-formedness bookkeeping

Constructors carry `checkFormula? = some ()` hypotheses exactly where the
lifting IH cannot recover translatability from the conclusion: cut formulas
(`mp`, `impIntro`), schema components, quantifier bodies (`allCounit`,
`exElim`, `exIntro`, `ctxSubst`).  Expect adjustments once the M3.3
induction actually runs; that is the authoritative test.

### CoreThm vs emission (decision from the M3 kickoff discussion)

M3 targets (a) a deep-embedded `CoreThm` relation, not (b) `.cor`-emission.
Discipline that keeps (a) honest: **every `CoreThm` constructor must be
stated exactly as schematic as its named `.cor` counterpart** — pinned
observation context and all (e.g. `Forall_ctx_*` facts pin one prefix
binder; do NOT state them depth-generically in Lean).  A depth-generic
constructor with no single Core name would hide exactly the infinite-family
risk M3 is supposed to surface.  Emission stays the M4 lint-mode follow-up.

### Why the M2 depth boundary should dissolve here (to verify in M3.2)

The M2 boundary ("arbitrary nested quantifier bodies need a context-stack
renderer or per-depth `Forall_ctx_*` names") is a feature of the
NESTED-TOWER observation convention: handwritten bridges observe a depth-k
fact through k+1 nested binders, so each depth pins a new statement shape.
Under the single-closure convention the `SubstEquiv`-soundness statement at
depth k observes over the FLATTENED context `C[Ψ ++ Γ]` through ONE binder:

- connective cases use the map-generic point axioms at `X := C[Ψ ++ Γ]`
  (e.g. `Forall_or_reindex_beta (X Y) (s : X × Final → Y)` — X is any type,
  including a product);
- the quantifier case is `Forall_reindex`, already Y-generic;
- atom-argument cleanup under the k-fold lifted map is a k-step chain of
  the SAME finite `fst/snd/pair` observations with bigger arguments.

Proof-term SIZE grows with depth; the AXIOM SET does not.  That is exactly
the finite-basis claim.  The `Forall_ctx_*` slice remains what it is: the
depth-1 instances needed by the current nested-tower printer, not the
general mechanism.

### Soundness proof plan (next step) and gaps already identified

Case analysis for `SubstEquivSoundStmt` (induction on `SubstEquiv`):

- **connectives/not**: closed by the `compXStep` engine (done generically).
- **atom/papp**: `Forall_sub2_reindex_beta` on both sides, then per-slot
  cleanup of `(embed u) ∘ (σk ∘ fst)` down to `embed (substCoreTerm k u)`,
  by induction on the projection index vs. the telescope.  GAP FOUND: the
  recursion produces the shape `(k ∘ snd) ∘ (pair f g ∘ s)` — the basis has
  `Forall_snd_pair_then_beta_*` (bare pair) and `Forall_fst_pair_comp_*`
  (fst with precomposition) but NO snd-with-precomposition sibling.
  Expect to add a small `Forall_snd_pair_then_comp_beta_*` family (and
  possibly a comp2 variant) to `beta_basis.cor` — shapes to be pinned by
  the proof, not guessed in advance (M1/M2 lesson).
- **quantifiers**: `Forall_reindex`/`Exist_reindex` for the outer shape;
  the IH (one binder over the FLATTENED context `X ×' newCtx`) meets
  `Forall_ForallCong` (two-binder tower) through the FUSION bridge —
  fuse, then `Forall_ctx_iff_reindex_beta` to push the associativity map
  through the iff, then `Forall_ForallCong`, then trans glue.  So the
  pinned two-binder ctx slice is precisely the fusion seam — not dead
  weight.  GAP (expected): the fusion axiom itself,
  `Forall_fuse (X Y) (m) : iff (term (Forall (X×Y) Final m))
  (term (Forall Y Final (Forall X (Y×Final) (m ∘ α))))` with α the
  associativity map — this is the "generic-depth Beck–Chevalley" design
  item M1 deferred; it is Y-generic, hence a finite addition.

## Expected new basis names (to be confirmed by the M3.3 induction)

All Y-generic, hence finite:

- `Vy`-lifted tautology bundle: K, S, contraposition, ∧/∨/↔ schemas
  (siblings of `Forall_exfalso` / `Forall_iffTrans_ctx`).
- ∀-counit at a general context: instantiate an inner `Forall X (X × Y)` at
  the context head (nothing in the current basis evaluates the inner ∀ at
  `v0`; `Forall_elim` is Y = Final only).
- Closure fusion / export bridge: `Vy (X × Y) (P ∘ fst) ↔ Vy Y ((Forall X Y
  P) ∘ fst)` plus the trivial base `Vy Final (P ∘ fst) ↔ Pred.term P` —
  these derive the nested-tower export by induction on Γ.
- Weakening slice (`WeakenEquiv`, the `snd`-generator mirror of
  `SubstEquiv`): connective/quantifier cases reuse the existing map-generic
  reindex axioms; only the atom-argument cleanup for `proj n ↦ proj (n+1)`
  and the collapse `snd ∘ ⟨t, id⟩ = id` (observed at PC) are new.
- Closure instantiation for `ctxSubst`: from `Vy (X × Y) m` conclude
  `Vy Y (m ∘ lift⟨t, id⟩)` (related to `Forall_pointEval` / `inst`, but at a
  term of the context rather than a global point).
